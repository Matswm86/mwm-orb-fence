# QA: MWM Orb Fence world-1 vertical slice (2026-10-06)

**Verdict: NEEDS-CHANGES.** Nothing blocks a sideload. Lint, load, logic test, CI, APK contents, licences and every screenshot pass, and a non-reading 4-year-old can clear level 1 and reach level 2 with no text and no adult. Two child-input bugs keep this from SHIP. On level 1, the onboarding hand silently undoes the child's direction choice. On a 16:9 phone with a camera cutout, two taps in the top-left field corner leave the level. The phone performance reading is also still missing.

Audited commit: `6406649` (HEAD of `main`; this report is the only later commit).

## 4. Touch targets and the lowered home disc (judged)

The camera-cutout shift is `dy = safe_top - 36`. The home hit rect becomes x 0-216, y 0 to 216+dy, and the field starts at y 256. So the hit rect reaches into the field whenever the cutout is deeper than 76 px in viewport px. On a 16:9 phone this happens because the field is anchored to the bottom; on 20:9 the field sits 480 px lower and never overlaps (14). At the bot's fake 120 px inset, the overlap is exactly **44 px** (y 256-300) over columns c0-c2, as the builder reported. `OfPlay._input` handles field touches without marking them handled, so the same touch also reaches the home disc's `_gui_input`. Probe (Xvfb, fake inset 120, level 1):
```
B touch at (150.0, 285.0): ghost visible=true, home guarding=false
B after release: walls 0 -> 1, home guarding=true, screen=play
B second tap same spot 0.7 s later: screen=map
```
**Judgement:** this is not acceptable as is (finding 2). The overlap itself is small. But one touch drives both the field and the home guard, so two wall taps in the top-left cells within 0.3-2.0 s leave the level, and the first tap already shows the gold guard ring for no visible reason. That breaks rule 20 (a home button must not fire by accident). Quitting costs nothing (GDD 8.6), so this is Medium, not a block.

## 5. Performance risk (static; no phone attached)

- **Draw calls:** about 30 in play, within DESIGN 7d's limit of 50. The count covers:
  - the sky, planet and moon cards, field glass and rim
  - 3 MultiMeshes (crystal, ice and stones, up to 224/224/32 instances), rails, rail lights and frame nodes
  - per ball: sphere, ring, halo, trail and Snegl quad
  - the beam, tips, tokens, and the 2D UI
- **Lights and effects:** 2 directional lights, shadows off. Glow is on (levels 1-3, additive, HDR threshold 1.0) and MSAA 3D is 2x. SSAO, SSR and fog stay at their defaults (off). There are no GPU particles; the pops and sparks are 2D `_draw` in `OfFieldFx`.
- **Textures:** the largest is the 1152x2048 sky; the pictures are 1008x1152. None is over 2048 px.
- **Transparent layers:**
  - blended: field glass, rim, ice MultiMesh, trails
  - additive: beam, glow quads, Snegl
  - the win card backdrop reads a full-screen mip-blurred screen texture (`blur_dim.gdshader`) while the card shows
- **Per-frame allocations:** small.
  - `OfSim._move_ball` (`OfSim.gd:584,602`) builds 2 typed arrays per axis per substep per ball, about 4 per ball per frame.
  - `OfFieldFx._round_rect` (`:298`) builds a format string per call to look up a cached StyleBox.
  - `OfSettings._draw` (`:205`) allocates a StyleBox, but only on redraw.
  - There is no `new()` or `instantiate()` in `_process`.
- **Fallback:** `rendering_device/fallback_to_opengl3 = true` (checked through ProjectSettings), so the 32-bit tablet without Vulkan should fall back to Compatibility. DESIGN 7d asks for this to be checked on the device. The manifest declares `android.hardware.vulkan.version` as required. That does not stop a sideload, but it would filter the tablet on Play.
- **PerfOverlay:** the autoload is registered and matches the studio template, except that the comment says "the owner" instead of the name. **Phone performance: UNVERIFIED.** Ask for fps, draws and VRAM from the phone and the tablet on level 3 mid-capture and on the level-clear reveal.

## 6. Look (premium stylized 3D): top 5 "prototype" tells, by visual gain per GPU ms

1. **The unchosen direction icon reads as a broken glyph** (01, 02a: "Φ"-like). `_outline_arrow` (`OfDirButtons.gd:100-114`) draws a shaft, a second shaft offset 7%, and a centre ring over both. The chosen icon is clean. Drop the offset shaft and the ring. Cost: 0 ms.
2. **The grid dots shout** (01-04). The intersection dots are 14:1 against the glass while the lines are 3.4:1 (sampled from 02a). The field reads as graph paper, not a force field. Dim the dots to about the line level. Cost: 0 ms.
3. **The moon card behind the field is a muddy grey blob** through the glass (01-04, 09). Lift its value or tint it toward the world colour, or move it off the field. Cost: 0 ms.
4. **The Snegl token is lost on a growing beam** (04, crop at x 660-920, y 1040-1280). The spiral barely shows under the beam glow. Draw the token over the beam, or give the spiral more contrast.
5. **The win card is a flat cream slab** (06, 16), and with no next disc (shell level 3) the replay and map discs sit off-centre with an empty third slot (16). Add a thin rim or glow edge, and centre the two discs. This is the same as Neon Bricks findings 3 and 5.

DESIGN section 10 budget: 0 shadowed lights, 0 particle systems, 7 transparent or additive materials, 0 textures above 2048, and SSAO/SSR/fog off. The section 10 must-keep holds: captured area shows bright faceted crystal with the picture and a white rim, while open field stays dark glass (04, 05).

## 8. Findings

1. **Medium:** `scripts/OfPlay.gd:293-296`. On level 1, until the child first touches the field, each loop of the onboarding hand sets `buttons.chosen` to the hand line's direction. This overrides the child's own tap on a direction disc. Probe: `child tapped button 1 -> chosen=1`, then `t=0.2s after tap: chosen flipped to 0 (hand visible=true)`. It reproduced in 2 of 2 runs. It also breaks GDD 3.3 and 8.3 ("Up-down is pre-chosen"): screenshot 01 starts on side-side because the hand chose a horizontal line. Fix: stop the hand once any direction disc is tapped. Or keep `chosen` and only `buttons.glow()` the hand's direction, the way `show_hint()` does.
2. **Medium:** `scripts/OfMain.gd:114-117` together with `scripts/OfPlay.gd:339-346`. On a 16:9 screen with a cutout deeper than 76 px, the lowered home hit rect covers the top 44 px of field cells c0-c2 (at a 120 px inset). One touch there both starts a field ghost and arms the home guard, and a second wall tap within 0.3-2.0 s opens the map (probe in section 4). Fix: call `get_viewport().set_input_as_handled()` in `OfPlay._on_press` when the touch lands in the field or on a direction disc. Or clip `home.size.y` to the field top and widen the rect sideways to keep 216 px of area.
3. **Low (spec):** `scripts/OfSettings.gd:49-79`. The settings pills are 360x120 px, under the studio floor of 130 px (adult screen). The sliders are 100 px tall. Raise the pills to 130 px or more; there is room, since the panel ends at y 1615.
4. **Low (spec drift):** `scripts/OfSettings.gd` against GDD 10.2 (`docs/GDD.md:369`). There is no vibration row, because the stand-alone build asks for no vibrate permission (`OfState.gd:29-30`). The README already matches the build, so update the GDD to match.
5. **Low:** `scripts/OfWinCard.gd:85-87`. With no next disc (shell level 3, 16), replay and map stay at x 270 and 540. Centre them at x 360 and 720 when `has_next()` is false.
6. **Low (look):** `scripts/OfDirButtons.gd:100-114`. The unchosen icon clutter described in section 6, item 1.
7. **Low (test tooling):** `tests/capture.gd:145-170`. The "turn the ghost" step taps side-side without checking the starting direction. In this run the hand had already chosen side-side, so 02a and 02b are the same picture and the check proves nothing. Before taking 02a, set `play.buttons.chosen = 0` and confirm the ghost is vertical. This step would also have caught finding 1.
8. **Low (test tooling):** `tests/capture.gd:56-61`. Shot `03_level1_gameplay` shows the win card: one wall cleared the level before the shot. Take the shot right after the first wall finishes, or use level 2.
9. **Info (perf):** `scripts/OfSim.gd:584,602`. There are 2 typed-array allocations per axis per substep per ball, about 4 per ball per frame. That is harmless at 1-3 balls. Reuse a member array when worlds 4-6 bring 8 balls.
10. **Info (flash):** the wall-complete shine, the milestone shimmer and the bounce sparks bypass `OfFlashLimiter`. They are small and local, so rule 37 still holds by design, but this has not been measured on screen.
11. **Info (store):** the manifest requires `android.hardware.vulkan.version` (`aapt2`: `uses-feature: ... vulkan.version version='4194307'`). Sideloading is fine. For a Play release, make it not-required so the OpenGL fallback devices can install.
12. **Info (size):** the 66-minute music track is 31.7 MB of the 185 MB APK. If size matters for Play or MWM Play, trim it to a loop of about 10 minutes.
13. **Info (test tooling):** every run ends with "ObjectDB instances leaked" and "2-6 resources still in use", as in Neon Bricks. Clear the statics before `quit()` so real leaks are not hidden.
