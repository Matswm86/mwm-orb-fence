# QA: MWM Orb Fence worlds 2-6 and Uendelig (2026-10-07)

**Verdict: NEEDS-CHANGES.** Nothing blocks a sideload:
- Lint, load, logic test, all 5 capture phases and CI are green.
- All 30 levels match GDD 6.3 in both settings. The Uendelig rules match 6.5.
- A random-tapping child clears every level I tried and every Uendelig round in Lett.

## 5. Performance risk (static plus engine counters; no phone)

Engine counters (`RenderingServer.get_rendering_info`, desktop Vulkan Mobile renderer). These are scene-structure counts, not phone timings:
```
L1  Lett 1 ball start          draws  72  objects 108  prims  7795
L16 Lett 4 balls, wall growing draws  75  objects 111  prims  9455
L16 Vanlig 9 balls start       draws 124  objects 162  prims 12605
L29 Vanlig 14 balls start      draws 174  objects 212  prims 18159
L29 Vanlig 14 balls mid, wall  draws 189  objects 229  prims 29335
L30 Vanlig 14 balls start      draws 125  objects 163  prims 28347
```
- **Draw calls are worse than the builder's estimate.**
  - The baseline is already 72 with one ball.
  - Each ball adds about 4 draws: sphere, ring, halo quad and trail (`OfWorld.gd:455-510`). Stor adds a second ring.
  - Each token is a GLB of 3 meshes plus a halo.
  - L29 adds the stone, mirror, mirror-face and out MultiMeshes.
- **Crystal:** `_mm_instance` sets `instance_count = 504` with no `visible_instance_count` (`OfWorld.gd:387, 556-566`). So 504 x 22 tris = 11k tris are submitted once the MultiMesh is in view, even on 14x16 (224 cells). The counter shows 7.8k prims on L1 before any capture, so the zero-scaled pool seems to be culled until the first crystal exists. I could not confirm the full 11k from the counters.
- **Other budget items:**
  - 2 directional lights, shadows off.
  - Glow is on (levels 1-3) and MSAA 3D is 2x.
  - No GPU particles.
  - The largest texture is 1152x2048; none is over 2048.
  - PerfOverlay is registered as an autoload.

**Risk for the 32-bit tablet: HIGH.** If that tablet falls back to OpenGL ES (`fallback_to_opengl3` is on), each draw is a driver call on a slow ARMv7 core. 150-190 draws plus additive halos and trails over the 504-cell field will likely miss 60 fps on L26-L30 Vanlig and on Uendelig Vanlig at the cap.

**Cheapest fix:** pool the ball parts into MultiMeshes:
- one for spheres, one for rings, one for halos
- per-instance colour or custom data for kind, Snegl and caged

That takes about 40 draws off L29 and keeps the count flat as balls grow. Then:
- set `crystal_mmi.multimesh.visible_instance_count = cols * rows` (and the same for ice) in `bind_level`. This is one line and cuts 280 idle instances on 14x16.
- if that is not enough, skip trails when there are more than 8 balls.

**Phone performance: UNVERIFIED.** Ask for fps, draws and VRAM from PerfOverlay (three-finger tap) on L29 Vanlig mid-capture and on Uendelig Vanlig round 12, on the phone and on the tablet.

## 6. Builder judgement calls

1. **Skjold on a ball pushes it sideways: acceptable.** I ran 300 trials over L26-30 in both settings, starting a shielded wall on a free ball's cell:
   ```
   SKJOLD trials 300 popped 0 unfinished-after-10s 0 ball-in-solid-after 0 fallback-teleports 10 pushed-to-other-side 10 max-jump 85 px
   ```
   There are no pops, no stuck walls and no ball left inside a wall. In 3% of trials the side push does not fit, and `_nearest_empty` moves the ball up to 85 px (under 2 cells), sometimes to the other side of the new wall. `_nearest_empty` (`OfSim.gd:857`) also ignores regions, while GDD 4.6 says "in its own region". This is Low (finding 6).
2. **L29 has 3 tokens against the GDD 5.2 "max 2" rule: a GDD contradiction, not a code bug.** Table 6.3 says "1 of each" for L29, and `OfBalance.MAX_TOKENS = 3` is commented. Fine for an 8+ "everything" level. Amend GDD 5.2 to "max 2, L29 has 3" (finding 5).
3. **Best-round star only in Vanlig: conforms.** GDD 6.5 says the HUD best star is "Vanlig only". The map's Uendelig disc shows the best round in both settings (`OfMapScreen.gd:116`).
4. **Builder-made maps 6-30: no unfair spawn found.** All open cells are connected, and spawns avoid solids and tokens. Lett bot and random child clear every level with 0 safety moves. Two notes:
   - L6's "corner stones" sit 3 cells in, not in the corners. Cage pockets still form with two walls.
   - L24 and L29 put mirrors 2 cells from an edge, which is fine.

## 7. Audio: `of_mirror` (not heard by anyone)

My numeric analysis (ffmpeg plus numpy; I did not listen, because there was no audio device):
```
of_mirror.ogg  44.1 kHz mono  0.360 s   true peak -2.7 dBFS  mean -22.2 dB  no clipping, no DC offset
spectrum peaks: 1864 Hz (main), 976 Hz, 940 Hz        envelope: 0.72 -> 0.10 in 70 ms, silent by 260 ms
```
- **Level:** it plays at -14 dB in the table (`OfSfx.gd:41`), so it sits under the other effects.
- **Rate:** it shares the 6-per-second bounce limit (`OfSfx.gd:137-143`). That matters, because the bot made 411 mirror turns in one L22 Vanlig run.
- **Pitch:** the pitch is `BALL_RADIUS / radius`. On 21x24 that is x1.2 for a plain ball (about 2.2 kHz) and x1.6 for a Kvikk ball (about 3.0 kHz). It is a short, bright glassy ping.
- **Wider effect:** the same rule raises every plain-ball bounce by x1.2 in worlds 4-6 and Uendelig from round 8 or 9.

**The owner must listen before release:** play L22 Vanlig (8 mirrors, 11 balls) on phone speakers and check the Kvikk pings on L24. If it is shrill, clamp the mirror pitch to 1.2 or base it on the 48 px grid reference.

## 8. Findings

1. **Medium (perf):** `scripts/OfWorld.gd:455-510` and `:387`. Measured draws: 174-189 on L29 Vanlig, 124 on L16 Vanlig, 72 at baseline. Both ceilings (DESIGN 50, studio 100) are exceeded, and the risk on the 32-bit tablet is high. Fix: use MultiMeshes for ball spheres, rings and halos; set `visible_instance_count` on the crystal and ice pools; then get the PerfOverlay reading (section 5).
2. **Medium (audio):** `assets/sfx/of_mirror.ogg` has not been heard by anyone, and the Kvikk pitch x1.6 puts it near 3 kHz (`OfSfx.gd:143`). The owner listens on a phone before release; clamp the pitch if it is shrill.
3. **Low (look, 21x24):** the ice-bar branch in `OfWorld.gd:756-761`. A finished wall between two live rooms draws as an 11 px grey bar (sampled x 486-497 in `p08_L18_settled`, (123, 136, 157) on (6, 18, 57)). The rest of the wall cell still shows glass and grid dots, so the wall reads as "a line" more than "a wall". Graphic-designer: widen it toward the full 48 px cell, or tint the cell.
4. **Low (look, 21x24):** 504 bright grid dots (`p03`, `p06`) make the 2026-10-06 "graph paper" note worse. Dim the dots, or draw every second one, on the 48 px grid. Cost: 0 ms.
5. **Low (spec):** `docs/GDD.md` 5.2 says "max 2 tokens per level", but 6.3 L29 says "1 of each" and the code allows 3. Amend 5.2.
6. **Low:** `scripts/OfSim.gd:433-437` and `:857-876`. The Skjold fallback and `_safety` use `_nearest_empty` over the whole grid by Manhattan distance, not the ball's own region (GDD 4.6). In 3% of shielded starts this means a jump of up to 85 px. Limit the search to the region (flood fill from the ball's cell), or accept and update GDD 4.6.
7. **Low (pacing):** Uendelig Lett from round 9 (21x24, 9-10 balls). The random-child median is 111-117 s per round (max 175 s). GDD 6.5 already notes 78 s for its bot. Watch the owner's child play one run; if it drags, cap Lett at 8 balls on 14x16.
8. **Low (Vanlig HUD):** `scripts/OfHud.gd:74`. The best-round digit is 24 px, under the 40 px text floor. The 39-42 spark stars are about 14 px each and cannot be counted (`21_w6_L29_vanlig_sparks`, `27_endless_r6_vanlig_hud`). This only affects Vanlig (8+), so it is a look note: raise the digit to 32-40 px and draw sparks in groups of 3 per ball.
9. **Info (test tooling):** the `worlds` capture phase does not shoot L6 (first level of world 2) or L16 (first 21x24 level). Add both, plus a tall-map shot with the Uendelig row. Every run still ends with "2-4 resources still in use at exit".
10. **Info:** the map pages for worlds 2-6 show 5 identical placeholder discs (accepted; the pictures will tell levels apart).
