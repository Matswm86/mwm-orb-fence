# Credits

## Art
All 3D models, textures, the sample hidden picture and the mockups in this repository were made for MWM Orb Fence with Blender 4.5 scripts and Python + Pillow (`docs/mockups/src/`). The planets, nebula and stars are procedural shaders; no photos or third-party image assets are used.

The hidden pictures for levels 1, 2, 4 and 5 and the app icon are drawn with Python + Pillow by `tools/make_pictures.py` and `tools/make_icon.py` (own work).

No Poly Haven, ambientCG or NASA assets are used. Third-party sounds are listed under Sound.
